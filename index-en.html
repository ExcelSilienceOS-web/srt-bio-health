Your photo is recorded to verify physical presence of the registered driver prior to vehicle key release.

2. Bioacoustic Voice Stress Analysis (5s)

The microphone measures laryngeal micro-tremors and vocal energy rate to evaluate autonomic nervous system fatigue and stress.
